I don't want your data, I don't want your photos!

oddbop runs entirely on your phone or computer. Your drawings, photos, band name and songs never leave your device, and nothing is sent to me.

What stays with you

  • Your drawings and photos are turned into your band right there on your device and saved in your browser. I never receive them, never see them, and can't get them back for you, so use Back up my band if you want a spare copy.
  • Songs you pick from your own device play from your device. They aren't uploaded anywhere.
  • A backup or a recorded clip is a file you save yourself. Where it goes next is up to you.

What I don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email address needed.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no cookies.
  • I don't collect anything about you, so there's nothing to sell, share or lose.

The small print, honestly

  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps basic visitor logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and safe. I don't see or use them.
  • oddbop's own songs stream from this website. That's the only thing your device fetches while you play.
  • The follow-us links open Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, which have their own privacy rules.

Share it!

Made something great? Feel free to share your clips on socials and tag @odd.bop.

Anything you post, including a face photo you put on a player, is then public on that platform.
